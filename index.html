What this impact area is measured by

The plan measures Training by one headline indicator: the share of employees who take part in sustainability activities or training in the year. It is scored by the Sustainability Tracking, Assessment & Rating System (STARS) of the Association for the Advancement of Sustainability in Higher Education, under its Engagement category (EN). Nine detailed indicators sit below it, each feeding the headline or a STARS credit. Years run April to March and are written as fiscal years (FY).
